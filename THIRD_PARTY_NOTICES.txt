log-record third-party notices
=============================

Scope and license separation
----------------------------
Original log-record code is covered by the root MIT LICENSE, which remains
unchanged. That MIT license does not relicense the Gradle Wrapper or any
external dependency. The three published library modules are ordinary
JAR/AAR artifacts, not shaded bundles of their runtime dependencies.
Consumers and distributors must retain the applicable notices of the
artifacts they actually redistribute.

This inventory reflects declared versions and inspected cached POM/license
files on 2026-09-13. It is not a complete inventory of every possible
consumer's resolved graph or of all build-tool dependencies.
